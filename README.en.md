# Okazuri BOT

[日本語](README.md) | English

A Discord bot that searches the magazines of the labels distributed on DLsite and the works of many sites at once.
Site: https://okazuri.nae.quest/ - [API documentation](https://okazuri.nae.quest/dev) - [search server (okazuri-search-api)](https://github.com/NaeCqde/okazuri-search-api)

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/search` | Searches by the title of a work and so on. The results are split into legal and illegal. You can search in Japanese, romaji or English. With `by_magazine` on, the work you typed is turned into the name of the issue that printed it, and that is searched. When the result is long, the rest can be read on the search page of the website. |
| `/magazine` | Finds the issues of the magazines that printed a work, from its title. Titles are suggested as you type. |
| `/works` | Lists the works of a magazine issue, from the name of the issue. |
| Message menu "Search this" | Reads the titles from the links to work pages in a message and searches them. "Search this with option" lets you choose whether to search by magazine. |

The [search page of the website](https://okazuri.nae.quest/search) searches several words at once and lets you choose the grouping and the sort.
